### BCGT-000402: Composable penalty mechanisms under side contracts
- **Subfield:** Proof markets, verification incentives and cryptographic mechanisms
- **Open:** Characterize when monetary penalties preserve incentives if outside contracts insure, hedge or reimburse slashing losses.
- **Status:** Research target connecting 2014 penalty protocols with Rapidash’s 2022–2025 exchange guarantees.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** External finance can invalidate local deterrence.
- **Key works:** Bentov and Kumaresan (2014), *How to Use Bitcoin to Design Fair Protocols*, IACR ePrint:2014/129; Chung et al. (2025), *Rapidash*, IACR ePrint:2022/1063.
- **Related:** Universal composability of incentives; insurance moral hazard.
